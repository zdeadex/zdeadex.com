Gymmy

Gym and studio software: schedule, members, payments, and a website.

Live hellogymmy.com ↗

Why I built it

I train in a lot of different gyms, and I kept running into the same frustrating experience in gym apps. So I built one complete package, with every tool a gym, a CrossFit box, or a pilates studio needs, and I used AI so it stays current with how people actually work.

Role

Founder, built everything

What it does

Gymmy’s site says it gives gyms time back with their members and runs operations, with a modular dashboard for owners and an assistant for members.

The owners page describes schedule, members, memberships, and payments in one place. The pricing page says the plan includes a website. The terms of service say Gymmy is online software published by StakeLab Pte. Ltd.: a dashboard for owners and coaches (schedule, programming, members, payments) and an assistant for members.

Key features

  • Class schedule with capped spots and a waitlist
  • Member profiles, attendance, and membership status
  • Memberships and payments, with card billing through Stripe and failed-payment follow-up
  • WODs and programming, including import from an existing document
  • Several gyms on one account
  • An assistant for schedule, members, and numbers
  • Analytics for attendance, class fill rate, and membership trends
  • Guided migration from other software
  • For members: book a class, waitlist alerts, today’s WOD with loads, scores and PR history
  • Website included, as stated on the pricing page
