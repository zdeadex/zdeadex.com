What it does

Paygram’s site says you launch a token, stock, or basket paired to any handle on Telegram, X, GitHub, or Fomo. You pick a @username; Paygram mints the token, routes creator fees in SOL, and pays 80% to that handle’s social-linked wallet. Unclaimed fees stay in the vault.

The fees page says Paygram adds no trade fee on pump.fun launches. Of the creator fee, 80% goes to the handle and/or holders and 20% buys and burns $PAYGRAM. Creating a coin has no Paygram fee; network fees still apply, and a dev buy is optional.

Key features

  • Launch a token, stock, or basket paired to a Telegram, X, GitHub, or Fomo handle
  • Creator fees paid in SOL to a social-linked wallet
  • 80% of the creator fee to the handle and/or holders; 20% buys and burns $PAYGRAM
  • Unclaimed fees stay in the vault
  • No Paygram launch fee and no extra Paygram trade fee on pump.fun, per the site
