What it does

Struvo’s site says: find and book a coach near you. Train in person, follow an online plan, or get monthly coaching.

The homepage headline is “Find your coach. Make your next move.” It lists in-person sessions, written online plans, and monthly coaching with check-ins. Search is by sport and area, then compare profiles and book a time.

The site’s FAQ says payments are not live yet and booking is not open yet. Browsing coaches and plans, and creating a free account, are available. It says Struvo is for adults 18 and over, and that every coach is reviewed before a profile is public.

Key features

  • In person: one-on-one or small group, at a gym, park, or studio, with prices and areas listed
  • Online plan: a written plan, week by week, one price shown before you buy
  • Monthly coaching: check-ins, feedback, sessions and reply times set by the coach
  • Search, compare, and book
  • Coaches are reviewed before they appear
  • The site says reviews are intended only from completed paid sessions
