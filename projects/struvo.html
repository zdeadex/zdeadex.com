What it does

Struvo’s site presents it as the trusted third party for the building trade. The page says it is not another directory: verified companies, certified reviews after a real job, and a selected network.

The headline is “Find a reliable professional, without stress or a bad surprise.”

Verification covers SIRET, Kbis, and insurance: an automatic OCR check against criteria, then a review. The site says this is not a simple online listing.

The Struvo badge is earned after seniority or several jobs, and a certified rating. A Premium badge is for companies that accept job-site audits.

Clients can upload before, during, and after photos. The site says those photos are timestamped and geolocated, and that only the client uploads them. It also mentions random or planned on-site checks.

How it works, as listed on the site: search verified companies rated on objective criteria, follow the job with a clear record, then leave a certified review after the real work.

For companies, the site describes a monthly subscription for visibility. The first five companies in each trade get one year free. The badge is then earned from proof and certified reviews.

Key features

  • Companies checked before they are listed
  • No anonymous reviews, and no reviews that are not tied to a job
  • Five objective quality criteria per review
  • SIRET, Kbis, and insurance checks, then a review
  • Badge after seniority or several jobs, plus a certified rating
  • Premium badge for companies that accept job-site audits
  • Client photos before, during, and after, timestamped and geolocated
